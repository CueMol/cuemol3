CueMol 3.0.5 (build 576)

The main changes:

- **Fast GI ambient occlusion (CueMol3)**: a new AO method that gives
  molecular surfaces, CPK and cartoons a stronger depth cue. It is the default
  for new scenes; scenes saved before keep their GTAO look.
- **Materials in the view (CueMol3)**: a renderer's material (metal, toon,
  plastic, the new ceramic, ...) now shows in the WebGL view, and the ray
  tracer is matched to it.
- **Translucent renderers composite correctly (CueMol3)**: overlapping
  translucent renderers no longer depend on the drawing order.
- **Density map level on its histogram (CueMol3)**: the contour level is a
  draggable marker on the map's density histogram.
- **Better movie quality (CueMol3)**: H.264 / H.265 movies are encoded by
  quality instead of a low fixed bit rate.

---

## Display (CueMol3)

- **Fast GI** (Scene inspector > Ambient occlusion > Method):
  - A screen-space AO that darkens only the ambient light, plus occlusion
    baked into a 3 A grid for large cavities. The bake reruns after the
    scene's geometry changes, not while the view rotates.
  - Fast GI only: **Indirect light** and **Baked occlusion** sliders.
  - New **Lighting > Brightness** scales all lights, with or without AO.
  - GTAO stays selectable. A scene file without the AO method loads as GTAO
    with the former defaults and keeps that look when resaved.
- **New scene defaults**: new scenes start with the High antialiasing preset
  and the Medium AO preset with AO on. Defaults you saved in the New Tab
  dialog are kept.
- **Materials**: each renderer is lit with its own material, and triangles are
  lit per pixel.
  - The default material is now matte plastic.
  - Metals reflect a sky gradient and tint their highlight with the surface
    colour.
  - toon1 / toon2 give flat tones close to POV-Ray when AO is off.
  - New **ceramic**: a sharp highlight with a bright rim.
  - Bright colours roll off to white instead of clipping.
- **Translucent renderers** are composited as the ray tracer does: each
  translucent renderer shows its front-most surface, overlapping ones blend
  independently of order, and labels set on top stay on top. A translucent
  shell no longer darkens the structure inside it under Fast GI.

## Density maps (CueMol3)

- The Density map pane's **Level** is an entry above the map's density
  histogram, with the level as a marker on it.
  - Drag the marker (Shift for fine steps, Esc to cancel) or click the strip
    to set the level; each change is one undo step.
  - Drag, pinch or scroll on the strip to pan and zoom; **- / + / Fit** set
    the range.
  - The entry is in the current unit (sigma, top percent or absolute); the
    other unit is shown below it.

## Rendering (CueMol3)

- **Movies**: H.264 / H.265 movies default to a quality setting (**Quality**:
  High / Standard / Small file). The previous default of 1024 kbps broke thin
  lines and outlines into blocks. A fixed bit rate can still be chosen under
  **Rate control** (default now 10240 kbps). WMV always uses the bit rate.
- **Ray-traced edge lines** (umbreon):
  - Edge lines no longer blink on and off while the scene rotates in an
    animation. The previous behaviour can be selected with the render setting
    `umbreon.screenEdgeDepth=legacy`, and `umbreon.screenEdgeJump` sets the
    smallest depth step drawn (default 0.5 A).
  - A second edge method draws outlines as 3D lines in the scene, matching the
    POV-Ray export: `umbreon.edgeMethod=object`. The default stays `screen`.
  - These settings have no GUI yet; set them from the console, e.g.
    `set_render_settings settings="umbreon.edgeMethod=object"`.
  - A coarse mesh can show short lines along its facets; raising the
    renderer's detail removes them.

## User interface (CueMol3)

- Focus rings are a 1 px line flush with the control, instead of a 2 px ring
  with a gap.
- Side-panel pane titles have a tinted band and a stronger separator, so the
  boundary between panes is easier to see.
